# Prose Detone Narrative and Lexical Catalogs

This page carries the two families that dominate card prose: the narrative/RP patterns (N) for openings, dialogue examples, and atmospheric worldbook entries, and the lexical families (L) used for search and clustering. The owning protocol is `references/30-prose-detone.md`; general patterns A–G live in `references/31-prose-detone-catalog.md`.

## Narrative Priority

When conflicts arise, protect in this order:

1. host protocol, verbatim-locked items, worldview, character card, worldbook, and system rules;
2. events that already happened: locations, items, injuries, time, and causal continuity;
3. user-character sovereignty and interaction boundaries;
4. what the viewpoint character can know, notice, and express;
5. each character's established goals, relations, attitudes, emotional intensity, line facts, and action intent;
6. user-enabled personal hard bans on surface structure;
7. other word preferences, scene pacing, imagery, and author choices;
8. ordinary tic removal and local prettiness.

Personal hard bans may change a sentence's surface frame but never items 1–5; on conflict with a verbatim lock, keep and report. Ordinary tic removal never changes items 1–7. When setup conflicts surface, fix continuity first; never mask problems with fluent sentences.

"Events that already happened" means only events in established legitimate canon — never unauthorized decisions, lines, feelings, or irreversible actions added for the user character inside the current draft; those stay sovereignty violations to delete or roll back.

## Scene Ledger Before Each Turn

Record internally in shortest form:

- current place, time, weather, visible objects;
- characters present and relative positions;
- each person's last action and unfinished actions from the previous turn;
- known information and what each character does not know;
- current goals, obstacles, risks, open questions;
- items, injuries, clothing, environment state;
- viewpoint, tense, narrative distance, and which characters may be controlled;
- voice anchors per character: lexicon range, sentence length, directness, avoided topics, habitual gestures.

The ledger serves continuity only; never restate it item by item in the prose.

## Hard Residue in RP

Usually delete on a single hit:

- OOC assistant talk such as "我理解你的设定", "接下来我会继续剧情";
- summarizing the user's turn sentence by sentence before entering the scene;
- delivery lines like "以下是续写", "希望符合你的预期";
- model rules, tokens, prompts, moderation, self-checks, or output formats leaking into prose;
- deciding the user character's thoughts, feelings, lines, or key actions without authorization;
- a character suddenly knowing information outside their viewpoint;
- inventing setup, consent, relationships, or backstory from nothing for safety or completeness.

If the interface protocol requires OOC and prose to coexist, use exactly the specified separator; never let OOC language pollute character prose.

## Narrative Tic Families

### N01 Turn Mirroring

- Signal: narration replays the user's just-written action in other words, producing no new reaction or consequence.
- Boundary: viewpoint switching needs re-explaining the same event, or the echo exposes a character's misunderstanding.
- Fix: start from the first new change; keep only the look-back that alters the viewpoint's meaning.

### N02 Emotion Naming Replacing Presentation

- Signal: every action explained as "这是愤怒、害怕、占有欲、自我保护".
- Boundary: the viewpoint character is consciously introspecting and has that verbal capacity.
- Fix: keep behavior, choice, or one viewpoint-close feeling; let the reader judge the rest.

### N03 Motivation Overreach

- Signal: narration asserting a character "真正想要、其实害怕、潜意识里知道" without setup or plot support.
- Boundary: omniscient narration or character setup explicitly provides it.
- Fix: write observable clues, hesitation, or conflicting choices; never state speculation as conclusion.

### N04 Generic Body-Reaction Stock

- Signal: different characters repeatedly sharing same-function throat, knuckle, finger, breath, eye-color, mouth-corner, heartbeat, and spine reactions; swapping body part, verb, or adjective still counts as the same stock.
- Boundary: the physiological reaction is important causality in the scene, or the work deliberately sets up body motifs.
- Fix: merge only repetitions with same object, same function, no development, and no independent consequence; keep every reaction with a different trigger, timing, or consequence. Never invent medical history, trauma, or habits to seem unique.

### N05 Gaze Carries the Whole Performance

- Signal: "眼底闪过、眸色一沉、目光复杂、目光落在/扫过" replacing concrete behavior and lines; eye verbs rotating without object change or consequence.
- Boundary: a restrained character genuinely interacting mainly through observation and gaze.
- Fix: give the gaze an object, a duration, or a choice it causes; delete if consequenceless.

### N06 Voice-Timbre Adjective Rotation

- Signal: every line tagged "低沉、沙哑、磁性、冰冷、温柔而坚定", or "声音里带着/透着" some abstract emotion.
- Boundary: volume or vocal state affects plot — through walls, injury, forced whispering.
- Fix: let line syntax and action show tone; label only sound traits that change how it is heard.

### N07 Microexpression Inflation

- Signal: several "微微、轻轻、微不可察、不易察觉" micro-movements stacked in one second; narrative time freezes, events stall.
- Boundary: extreme close narration, suspense beats, or a character observing a lie.
- Fix: merge micro-movements sharing one function without independent consequence; keep every change that alters observation, choice, or timing; then advance action or dialogue.

### N08 Air and Time Freezing

- Signal: "空气凝固、时间静止、世界安静、这一刻被无限拉长" or sourceless light changes used for ordinary pauses.
- Boundary: supernatural setup, major turns, or a consistent time-imagery system across the work.
- Fix: write what is actually heard or seen during the pause, and who breaks it.

### N09 "As If" Uplift

- Signal: comparisons like "仿佛整个世界只剩……", "似乎某种无形力量……" offering no new sense, scale, or judgment; swapping "宛如/犹如/像是" keeps the same function.
- Boundary: limited viewpoint needing an uncertain metaphor, with imagery from the character's experience.
- Fix: delete generic comparisons; use a concrete referent the character would think of, or stop at the fact.

### N10 Sensory Checklist

- Signal: every scene clocking in with sight, sound, smell, touch in order, senses unrelated to action.
- Boundary: establishing new environments, restricted senses, or clues hidden in the environment.
- Fix: three-plus senses in one paragraph only enters review, never auto-deletion; write only what the viewpoint now notices, misreads, or uses.

### N11 Emotion-Body One-to-One Translation

- Signal: every emotion word trailed by a fixed body-reaction family — "愤怒=指节/手指收紧、紧张=呼吸一滞/急促"; near-synonym action rotation is not variety.
- Boundary: the character has a stable established gesture already set up in text.
- Fix: let reactions be shaped by person, scene, and goal; never reskin with synonymous actions. Non-volitionality explicitly written in existing prose is action semantics — never trim it to neutral or volitional just because the words are common.

### N12 Essay-Style Dialogue

- Signal: characters fully defining concepts, listing reasons, and summarizing relationships in pressure, conflict, or intimate scenes.
- Boundary: the character's profession, personality, or scene is exactly formal statement, interrogation, or teaching.
- Fix: keep the core the person would truly say; let omissions, misunderstandings, interruptions, and actions carry the rest — without randomly creating grammar errors.

### N13 Everyone an Emotion Analyst

- Signal: all characters accurately naming their own and others' psychological mechanisms, communicating in equally mature therapy-speak.
- Boundary: professional counseling scenes, or specific trained characters with consistent setup.
- Fix: limit understanding by each character's knowledge, avoidance, and interests; allow characters to misjudge.

### N14 Name-Calling Overdensity

- Signal: two-person dialogue with nearly every line calling the other's name to manufacture intimacy or emphasis.
- Boundary: shouts, identity confirmation, group disambiguation, or a character's special habit.
- Fix: omit when unambiguous; keep names only where the name itself changes tone or power relation.

### N15 Symmetric Repartee

- Signal: both speakers trading equal-length, structure-rhyming, screenshot-ready comebacks, as if one author wrote golden lines for both sides.
- Boundary: comedic banter, ritualized negotiation, or highly rhetorical characters.
- Fix: make goals, information, and expressive ability asymmetric; let someone evade, misunderstand, interrupt, or act directly.

### N16 One Question, One Lecture

- Signal: a simple question triggering full background, motivation, and relationship summary — characters volunteering the information the reader needs.
- Boundary: confession, farewell, formal explanation, or a long-accumulated disclosure.
- Fix: answer only what the character is willing and presently forced to answer; reveal the rest through later conflict.

### N17 Relationship Summary Loop

- Signal: after every intimacy or conflict beat, summarizing "他们之间的关系已经改变/更进一步".
- Boundary: chapters crossing time spans, or relationship state genuinely affecting later choices.
- Fix: show change through the next behavior, form of address, distance, or risk taken.

### N18 Fast Healing of Conflict

- Signal: conflict barely appears before precise communication, mutual understanding, gentle catching, and growth uplift.
- Boundary: the relationship and plot are genuinely ready for resolution, or the genre seeks low-conflict comfort.
- Fix: keep costs, misunderstandings, residual disagreement, and character limits; never force closure for a "complete turn".

### N19 Automatic Moralizing

- Signal: a concrete action immediately explained with "这意味着/真正重要的是/这不仅是" into respect, growth, choice, love, or humanity's meaning; similar abstract landings repeating across paragraphs.
- Boundary: the work itself employs a commentary narrator sustained throughout.
- Fix: let consequences and character choices carry theme; a single fully synonymous uplift may be deleted, three-plus same-type closures across paragraphs count as a strong signal.

### N20 Mandatory Scene-Closing Golden Line

- Signal: every turn ending with "而这一次……", "他们都知道……" — foreshadowing, relationship summary, or uplift; multiple paragraph ends all complete judgments.
- Boundary: serialized chapter-style writing with explicit hook design.
- Fix: endings may stop on action, sound, space change, choice, question, or natural incompletion; hooks must come from new information — never add an abstract tail for a "complete turn".

### N21 Mechanical Escalation Loop

- Signal: every turn escalating "试探—反应—加码—更强反应" with no turns, failures, or external change.
- Boundary: the scene is exactly a short race, and escalation changes strategy or risk.
- Fix: introduce choices created by existing goals: retreat, detour, pause, misjudgment, interruption, or bearing consequences. Never add events from nothing.

### N22 Intimate-Scene Action Checklist

- Signal: actions advancing mechanically by generic body part and intensity order; character difference, space, and emotional decision vanish.
- Boundary: clear actions genuinely needed for continuity and consent boundaries.
- Fix: keep necessary position and boundary information; let pacing come from character goals, reactions, pauses, and environment — not item-by-item clocking.

### N23 Consent and Boundary Restatement

- Signal: already-established consent or relationship state re-certified by narration after every action, sounding like policy copy.
- Boundary: when boundaries change, waver, withdraw, or need re-confirmation, express clearly.
- Fix: keep confirmations that change choices; stable background needs no per-sentence explanation — but never delete actual boundary signals.

### N24 Appearance Stock Rotation

- Signal: every character appearance re-scanning eyes, lips, hair, figure with the same adjective set.
- Boundary: first meetings, disguise changes, shifted viewpoint relations, or appearance as clue.
- Fix: write only features noticed now and affecting judgment.

### N25 Environment Serving Emotion

- Signal: weather, light, curtains, wind, and dust always precisely mapping character emotion without physical effect.
- Boundary: symbolism established beforehand, or environment genuinely changing action.
- Fix: let environment keep its own logic; it carries emotion only when the viewpoint character actively associates.

### N26 Viewpoint Overreach Explanation

- Signal: limited viewpoint suddenly entering another's mind, or knowing unseen, unheard, untold information.
- Boundary: the narrative viewpoint explicitly switched or was omniscient from the start.
- Fix: return to observable signs or switch explicitly; never smuggle facts with "仿佛知道".

### N27 Characters Delivering the Author's Setting

- Signal: characters stating worldbuilding both already know, only to brief the reader.
- Boundary: the other party does not know, characters dispute interpretive rights, or the recitation itself reveals stance.
- Fix: embed setting in action consequences, misunderstandings, or real information gaps.

### N28 Narration Repeating Dialogue

- Signal: the line already expresses attitude, then narration re-explains it abstractly.
- Boundary: narration deliberately revealing the gap between line and true intent.
- Fix: delete when no gap; with a gap, write observable tells or explicit interiority.

### N29 Tone Adverbs Replacing Voice

- Signal: heavy "冷冷地、淡淡地、认真地、坚定地" around lines that are otherwise indistinguishable.
- Boundary: one sentence whose meaning flips with tone.
- Fix: differentiate through word order, address, information selection, and action; keep only crucial disambiguating adverbs.

### N30 Over-Accommodating Narrator

- Signal: narration constantly explaining risks, soothing feelings, and guaranteeing characters' goodwill for the reader, weakening conflict and ambiguity.
- Boundary: children's texts, comfort genres, or an explicitly gentle narrator.
- Fix: never guarantee how the reader should understand; let characters carry goodwill or consequences through behavior.

### N31 Default Transition Words (C)

- Signal: paragraphs frequently launched with "就在这时、与此同时、话音刚落、话音未落"; different scenes all manufacturing momentum with the same phrases.
- Boundary: two action lines truly simultaneous, or the timing between speech and new event affecting causality.
- Fix: write the new action and its trigger directly; keep transition words only when the time relation is invisible from sequence.

### N32 Planning Scaffold Leakage (H)

- Signal: internal planning labels — "用户输入整理、场景设置、角色状态快照、内心预演、风格检查、开始思考、确认输出语言" — appearing in player-visible prose.
- Boundary: the interface protocol explicitly requires status bars or debug display; even then, internal reasoning and player state stay separate.
- Fix: delete internal plans; output only protocol-permitted status fields and character prose. Never rewrite hidden thinking as narration to keep leaking.

### N33 Status Bar Crowding Narrative (C)

- Signal: panels like "关键记忆、世界主线、本回合变动、当前任务" repeating unchanged content every turn; prose becomes a system log.
- Boundary: presets, frontend, or the user explicitly request a fixed status bar whose fields serve memory or interaction.
- Fix: obey the host protocol strictly; when incremental updates are allowed, write only changes; when full dumps are required, never let panel language enter prose.

### N34 Decoration Layer Relay (S)

- Signal: one short scene densely deploying several families — voice timbre, gaze, mouth corner, body reaction, touch, frozen atmosphere, light, abstract comparison; or one simple action sequentially stacked with micro-adverbs, vague emotion, and abstract uplift. Each sentence reads fine alone; together they add no equivalent information.
- Boundary: "three-plus families in one scene" is a prefilter, not a hard threshold. Extreme close narration deliberately slowing at key beats stays, when each family separately provides perceptible new information, different consequence, or a developing motif.
- Fix: first list the facts, positions, risks, information, and choices this beat actually changes; keep only senses or actions serving those changes. Assign one primary rule and repair once — never rewrite per hit, nor replenish layers with another ornamental set.

### N35 Synonym Imagery False Change (C)

- Signal: adjacent paragraphs rotating the same emotion into 涟漪、波澜、潮水、暖流、漩涡, or the same gaze into 深邃、幽深、晦暗; words change, narrative function does not.
- Boundary: the work has established an image system and each recurrence changes scale, direction, relation, or character understanding.
- Fix: use a stable name for one motif and let it develop; where no development exists, restore concrete events or choices, or delete — never reskin with near-synonyms.

### N36 Micro-Adverb Rhythm Flattening (C)

- Signal: characters constantly acting "微微、轻轻、缓缓、悄然、微不可察"; all characters appear equally restrained, slow, or muted.
- Boundary: amplitude, speed, sound, or perceptibility changes contact, exposure, boundaries, or physical results; the character has an established movement rhythm.
- Fix: delete only degrees the verb and situation already fully express; existing amplitude, speed, sound, and perceptibility are each semantic locks — never infer new hesitation or choice, nor substitute another adverb.

### N37 Default Contact Choreography (C)

- Signal: different relationships and characters all using "指尖/手指/掌心 + 轻轻/缓缓 + 划过/滑过/拂过/摩挲/覆上" default contact; touch description changes no space, boundary, goal, or choice.
- Boundary: medical examination, caregiving, tactile navigation, object handling, established character habits, and any contact that changes consent, stopping, or uncertainty.
- Fix: write only contact needed for spatial continuity, interaction boundaries, or character purpose; delete decorative caresses. Never soften real coercion into willingness, and never add new reactions for the user character.

## Distinguishing Character Voices

Do not force catchphrases onto every character. Extract difference from existing text along these dimensions:

- Information selection: whether the character notices people, objects, rules, danger, status, or outcomes first;
- Syntax: short or long answers, subject omission, full causation or not;
- Directness: command, probe, hint, evasion, rhetorical question;
- Lexicon sources: profession, era, education, region, relationship circle;
- Emotion strategy: outward display, suppression, conversion into action, conversion into jokes, topic deflection;
- Power posture: who asks, who defines the question, who may stay silent, who explains;
- Knowledge boundary: which concepts the character can grasp, what they will misunderstand;
- Body habits: keep only established gestures related to choices.

With insufficient samples, maintain only explicit constraints; never fabricate a complete voice model.

## Scene Rewrite Flow

### 1. Mark New Information

Distinguish what the user's turn already contained from this turn's genuinely new reactions, actions, lines, and environment changes. Delete replays without viewpoint value.

### 2. Check Sovereignty

Confirm no key thoughts, feelings, lines, or irreversible actions decided for the user character. Necessary physical reactions stay inside protocol-permitted range.

### 3. Check Continuity

Verify item by item: positions, held objects, clothing, injuries, time, forms of address, characters present, last actions. Smooth language never offsets continuity errors.

### 4. Clean Explanation Layers

For each psychological explanation ask: does the event or line already express it? does it come from the current viewpoint? Delete the repetition or overreach answering yes/no.

### 5. Clean Action Stock

Merge only actions with same object, same function, no development, and no independent consequence; same-type actions at different timings or with different consequences all stay. When actions are interchangeable across characters, prefer deleting the functionless layer — never invent new behaviors unattached to character goals.

### 6. Move the Scene Forward

Every paragraph changes at least one thing: information, position, relationship risk, goal, choice, or environment. Pure atmosphere paragraphs may exist but must not consecutively replace events.

### 7. Recheck Dialogue

Delete background explanation, psych jargon, and summary lines the character would not say. Read aloud: main characters should be distinguishable by word order and information selection alone.

### 8. Choose a Natural Stop

No forced summary, uplift, or foreshadowing. Stop on the real action, question, choice, or sensory change this turn produced.

## RP False-Positive Protection

Never auto-delete just because something is common:

- recurring imagery the work has established;
- a character's fixed gestures or forms of address;
- explicit statements needed for accessibility, continuity, or interaction boundaries;
- deliberate poetic, archaic, theatrical, or slow pacing;
- repetition, interruption, and incomplete sentences in conflict scenes;
- consent, stop, or uncertainty signals in intimate scenes that change action;
- user-designated viewpoint markers, status bars, variables, macros, or frontend rendering tags.

The test is not "another model would also write this line", but whether it carries a unique function for this character, this moment, this place.

## RP Final Check

- Did this turn add events, or only restate and explain?
- Does each character know only what they can know?
- Is user-character sovereignty preserved?
- Can the actions happen continuously in space?
- Do lines fit character goals and expressive ability?
- Does psychological explanation exceed viewpoint evidence?
- Any interchangeable body/gaze/voice/atmosphere kits?
- Any auto-resolved conflict, uplifted relationship, or conclusions drawn for the reader?
- Does the ending come from the scene, not the model's "turn closure" reflex?

Any failure gets a local repair first — never rewrite the whole passage into another uniform literary voice.

# Lexical Families: Ornament, Synonym Clusters, and Controls

This module handles "no obvious factual error, but decorative ornament, synonym rotation, and fixed lyric kits make the text read model-shaped". It applies to all Chinese text; L05–L13 are most common in fiction, RP, and emotional expression and must be used together with the narrative rules above.

The goal is not eliminating literary flair. What gets removed is portable ornament independent of character, scene, argument, or object. An image coming from the current viewpoint, changing understanding, participating in causality, or developing across the text is protected; a word, however ornate, stays when accurate and irreplaceable.

## Deliberate Flair vs Automatic Ornament

Ask six questions in order:

1. Object-binding: could this description belong only to the current character, object, scene, or argument? If it works unchanged on another topic, risk rises.
2. Novelty: does it bring new sense, action, scale, judgment, or relation — instead of repeating "很有感觉"?
3. Causality: does it affect later choices, misunderstandings, actions, or argument?
4. Perspective: does the comparison come from experience the speaker or viewpoint character could have?
5. Development: does a recurring image change, or merely rotate 涟漪 into 波澜、潮水、漩涡?
6. Precision: would deletion or conversion to plain statement lose irreplaceable meaning, rhythm, or character voice?

If the first three are all no and it clusters with other word families in the same paragraph, prioritize it. If six is yes, keep it or only reduce density.

## Five Dispositions, Not One Flat Banlist

| Mark | Meaning | Default trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| delete | hard residue with no prose function standing alone | single | delete directly; check sentence breaks |
| rewrite | structure the user explicitly dislikes, or high-confidence template | single or user threshold | rewrite the carrying structure, preserve all semantics |
| limit | natural words forming model tone in clusters | local density | merge same-object, same-function, undeveloped repetitions; keep all independent information |
| observe | broad, high-false-positive, or domain-biased families | co-occurrence with other patterns | mark only; never edit on the word alone |
| protect | terms, quotes, brand lines, character motifs | any | keep as-is unless the user lifts protection |

Priority is always: host protocol and verbatim protection → facts, logic, character intent, and semantic locks → personal hard bans on surface structure → other voice and domain rules → ordinary density rules. `rewrite` is not emptying the matched characters; `limit` is not a fixed whole-text quota.

## Lexical Family Catalog

Family members serve search and clustering only — never a verbatim deletion list. After a hit, still run the object-binding, novelty, causality, perspective, development, and precision checks.

The L series provides search evidence only. General text is judged mainly by D/E/F rules; narrative/RP mainly by N rules; one fragment gets one `primary_id` performing one merged repair, other hits recorded as auxiliary signals — never rewritten once per label.

L05–L13 default down to `observe` outside RP, in medical, technical, and factual records. They must rely on collocation or syntactic anchors; bare words like "低沉、冰冷、流淌、被击中、复杂、某种" never trigger changes alone.

### L01 Negation-Affirmation-Uplift Frame

- Search members: 不是/并非……而是/而在于，不仅/不只是……更/而且，真正……的是，与其……不如，本质上、归根结底.
- Cluster signal: ordinary statements repeatedly wrapped as correction, truth-reveal, or high-low hierarchy; X/Y mostly abstract evaluations.
- Protection: factual exclusion, definitional disambiguation, debate rebuttal, rhetoric the character deliberately insists on.
- Fix: identify the real logic, then convert to positive statement, independent negation, priority, or evidenced coordination; never delete only the connector.

### L02 Decorative Comparison Triggers

- Search members: 仿佛、宛如、犹如、好似、像是、如同; "似乎/好像" joins this family only when actually introducing a comparison.
- Cluster signal: comparison targets abstract, sourceless, sensorily empty; removing the trigger leaves the same fact; several triggers in one paragraph maintaining "literary feel".
- Protection: real uncertainty in limited viewpoint; comparisons that make shape, sound, motion, or scale more precise; established motifs.
- Fix: keep the concrete difference the comparison brings; otherwise stop at fact, action, or sense. Never swap within the family.

### L03 Vague Feeling and the Unspeakable

- Search members: 说不出的、难以言喻、难以形容、莫名、某种、微妙、复杂、难辨.
- Cluster signal: continuously claiming complex feeling without conflict source, behavioral consequence, or perspective limit.
- Protection: the character truly cannot identify the feeling and this speechlessness affects action; suspense needing the unnamed.
- Fix: carry it with conflict choices, bodily limits, or concrete judgments already present; delete when ungroundable and functionless.

### L04 Liquid, Ripple, and Flowing Emotion

- Search members: 泛起、漾开、荡开、涌起、流淌、涟漪、波澜、潮水、暖流、漩涡.
- Cluster signal: every emotion "泛起" in the heart; paragraphs only rotate water imagery; imagery affects neither space nor character experience.
- Protection: waterside scenes, character memory, or an existing tidal motif; each recurrence genuinely develops.
- Fix: merge undeveloped repetitions, keep every recurrence advancing the motif; elsewhere restore triggering events, choices, or observable reactions.

### L05 Heart and Impact Reactions

- Search members: 心头一紧、心中一动、心弦被拨动、心跳漏拍、胸口发紧、被击中、被触动、悸动.
- Cluster signal: different emotions and characters sharing one visceral reaction with no action change after.
- Protection: physiological reactions constituting risk, symptom, or plot causality; stable established body habits.
- Fix: merge same-function consequenceless repetitions; keep every reaction with different trigger, timing, or consequence; never swap to another organ or another kind of tremor.

### L06 Micro, Slow-Motion, and Silent Adverbs

- Search members: 微微、轻轻、缓缓、悄然、默默、微不可察、不易察觉、几不可察.
- Cluster signal: every action shrunk, slowed, or muted; characters and pacing uniformly processed.
- Protection: amplitude, speed, or sound changing physical outcome, exposure, or others' perception.
- Fix: delete degrees visible from the verb; check amplitude, speed, and sound separately — never interchangeable.

### L07 Eye and Gaze Stock

- Search members: 深邃的眼眸、眼底闪过、眸色一沉、目光复杂、眼神锐利/灼热/幽深、眸中掠过.
- Cluster signal: eyes carrying the entire performance; every psychological change announced by eye color and light.
- Protection: observation, seeing through, gaze avoidance, or visual clues really changing the interaction.
- Fix: give the gaze a concrete object and consequence, or let lines, posture, and choices carry emotion; never swap to other eye words.

### L08 Mouth Corners, Lip Lines, and Standard Smiles

- Search members: 嘴角微微上扬、唇角勾起、扬起一抹笑意、笑意加深、唇线抿紧、苦涩一笑.
- Cluster signal: every attitude change landing on mouth corners; smiles varying only adjectives with no social or action consequence.
- Protection: whether the smile is seen, misread, or used to hide changes the relationship; established expression habits.
- Fix: merge same-function consequenceless mouth-corner stock; keep every expression changing the other's judgment at different timings; let lines or actions happen directly otherwise.

### L09 Breath, Throat, Fingers, and Spine Stock

- Search members: 呼吸一滞/变得急促，喉结滚动，指节泛白，手指收紧，脊背发僵，身体一颤.
- Cluster signal: body parts entering in stock order; narrative time freezes; reactions interchangeable across characters.
- Protection: injury, oxygen shortage, concealed movement, spatial contact, or physiological limits affecting next action.
- Fix: merge same-function consequenceless reactions; keep all body information with independent trigger, timing, or consequence and advance the scene; never backfill with another body part.

### L10 Voice-Timbre Adjective Rotation

- Search members: 低沉、沙哑、磁性、清冷、冰冷、温柔而坚定、带着不容置疑、尾音发颤.
- Cluster signal: every line carrying an abstract timbre label; remove labels and all characters speak identically.
- Protection: volume, distance, injury, disguise, or vocal manner changing who hears and how it is understood.
- Fix: differentiate voice through word order, address, information selection, pauses, and action; keep only crucial disambiguating labels.

### L11 Air, Time, Light, and Dust

- Search members: 空气凝固、时间静止、世界安静、光影交错、光晕笼罩、尘埃起舞、氤氲.
- Cluster signal: ordinary pauses collectively staged by the environment; light, wind, and time only rendering for the characters.
- Protection: supernatural rules, physical light sources, visibility, deadlines, or environmental clues really affecting action.
- Fix: write what actually happens or is noticed during the pause and who breaks it; delete stage lighting without physical source.

### L12 World, Fate, and Grand Witness

- Search members: 命运的齿轮、世界只剩彼此、宇宙回应、时光长河、灵魂深处、无形的力量.
- Cluster signal: local events auto-uplifted to worldly, fateful, or soul-level meaning without buildup.
- Protection: the work's worldview truly contains fate mechanics, or a commentary narrator sustains the same scale.
- Fix: return to the involved characters' knowable range and actual costs; grand conclusions need preceding accumulation, never one noun's work.

### L13 Light Touch, Warmth, and Softening Kits

- Search members: 轻轻触碰、暖意蔓延、柔软角落、被温柔包裹、融化防线、细微触电感.
- Cluster signal: all relationships processed by the same soft-focus touch; conflict, boundaries, and character difference vanish.
- Protection: touch, temperature, or contact boundaries are factual conditions of the current action.
- Fix: keep position, temperature, contact, and consequence; delete packaging prescribing feelings for the reader.

### L14 Paired Adjectives and Pseudo-Complexity

- Search members: 温柔而坚定、克制又汹涌、熟悉又陌生、脆弱却坚强、平静中带着复杂.
- Cluster signal: one pair of antonymous or near-antonymous adjectives declaring "complex" without behavioral contradiction or evidence.
- Protection: both traits have referable behaviors and the tension is the paragraph's focus.
- Fix: write how the two forces lead to different choices; without evidence, keep the more accurate one.

### L15 Rare Lyric Words and Four-Character Finishes

- Search members: 旖旎、悸动、氤氲、油然而生、难以言喻、不可名状、恍若隔世、命运交织.
- Cluster signal: the words alone carry all atmosphere; several ready-made four-character beats in one paragraph with interchangeable characters and objects.
- Protection: accurate meaning fitting era/character/genre, or stable vocabulary of the work.
- Fix: never replace "rarer equals higher-class"; delete uninformative items, converting to concrete relations or objects from the input when needed.

### L16 Cross-Domain Migration of Promo, Healing, and Literary Words

- Search members: evenly moving "赋能、重塑、疗愈、看见、拥抱、奔赴、共鸣、沉浸" from one domain into every text.
- Cluster signal: one piece simultaneously carrying counseling-report, healing-blogger, and literary-narrator vocabulary; the real register taken over by a mixed template.
- Protection: professional terms, long-term brand voice, or characters with clear lexicon sources.
- Fix: settle the primary register first, then restore that domain's concrete actions and stable terms; never swap in another trendy set.

### L17 Metaphor-Explanation-Uplift Triad

- Search members: metaphor immediately followed by "这意味着/这不仅是/真正重要的是", closing on abstract value.
- Cluster signal: the same information said once feelingly, once rationally translated, once value-uplifted.
- Protection: the explanation genuinely limits metaphor misreading, or the argument needs a new conclusion from the example.
- Fix: keep the more effective of metaphor and explanation; delete uplift adding no proposition.

### L18 Synonym-Modifier Chains and Near-Synonym Roulette

- Search members: one object rotated through "深邃、幽深、晦暗", "轻轻、微微、缓缓", or "波澜、涟漪、潮汐".
- Cluster signal: surface vocabulary rich, semantic role identical; avoiding repetition causes concept, intensity, or character-voice drift.
- Protection: each word corresponds to a different scale, stage, or observer, and the difference can be stated.
- Fix: stable naming for one concept; subtract functionless modifiers; where difference is needed, write action, scale, or consequence — never mine synonyms to fill holes.

### L19 "一丝/一抹" Emotion Measure Words

- Search members: 一丝、一抹、些许、几分 + 笑意、苦涩、温柔、复杂、慌乱、不安、暖意、光芒.
- Cluster signal: every emotion sliced into the same micro-unit and bound to gaze, mouth-corner, or timbre stock; character difference reduced to the trailing noun.
- Protection: degree difference changing whether a character notices or how they respond, or the measure belonging to stable narrative voice.
- Fix: delete degrees visible from action and context; when a micro-difference truly matters, keep the one most affecting observation or choice — never swap measure words.

### L20 "不容置疑/拒绝" Authority Seals

- Search members: 不容置疑、不容拒绝、不容反驳、不容抗拒、不容置喙、带着命令意味.
- Cluster signal: narration repeatedly declaring absolute authority for lines unsupported by wording, status, action, or consequence; different characters sharing one dominance label.
- Protection: law or rules really excluding choice; the relationship and on-scene power making coercion real; boundaries and risks that must not be softened.
- Fix: show power through line content, permission, action, or defiance consequences; never soften real coercion into willingness for the sake of detoning, and never add threats from nothing.

### L21 Non-Volition and Surprise Never Mix

- Search members: 不由自主、下意识、不自觉、不禁、无意识; "竟/竟然" is treated separately as a surprise modality, not folded into this family.
- Cluster signal: narration repeatedly declaring actions non-volitional, using labels to skip trigger, hesitation, and choice.
- Protection: non-volitionality explicitly asserted by existing prose is itself a semantic lock; medical, legal, psychological states, or physical reflexes need precise preservation. The expectation-gap of "竟/竟然" must also stay.
- Fix: when writing new text, never generate non-volition labels without setup or event basis. When cleaning existing prose, restructure only with the input's own triggers, reflexes, or equivalent wording; with no safe equivalent, keep the original. Never convert non-volition to neutral or volitional, and never delete surprise modality.

## Correct Use of Synonym Clusters

Synonym clusters discover "one template reskinned"; they never drive automatic replacement. In audit, label each word's function in its sentence, for example:

- degree shrinking;
- non-volition declaration;
- vague emotion;
- decorative comparison;
- body reaction;
- atmosphere uplift;
- abstract summary.

When different words consecutively carry one function, treat them as one repetition problem. Repair the function chain: delete repeated layers, restore agent and action, keep necessary differences. Swap a word only when the original is semantically inaccurate — and the replacement never comes from the same automatic roulette.

## Lexical Final Check

- Are personal `rewrite` items safely restructured, or explicitly recorded as conflicts under verbatim protection or other invariants? Were they replaced by near-synonymous structures?
- Is every handled negation, modality, scope, and causality still complete?
- Does the text keep at least its genuinely unique, developing imagery?
- Are multiple characters' actions, gazes, and voices separated from one shared stock?
- Any "for plainness" flattening — every sentence short, every emotion stated directly, syntax stripped of hierarchy?
- Any new detail, motive, bodily state, or character habit absent from the original?
- Are quotes, code, terms, host tags, and user verbatim-locked items byte-for-byte intact?

If after cleaning only uniform, correct, object-less sentences remain, the editor's voice has replaced the original; return to minimal sufficient change and restore protected specificity and rhythm.
